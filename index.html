About the Artist

About Quan Yuan

Art is a way of observing, interpreting, and expressing the world around us.

Quan Yuan is an artist whose work explores the relationship between observation, imagination, and visual expression.

Through painting and drawing, his work focuses on detail, atmosphere, composition, and the unique character of each subject.

This website presents a selection of his artworks and creative projects.
